Subsidized exports — the buffer is being exported

US oil exports surged +2.5 mb/d since the war began, hitting a record 12.9 mb/d. Not from new production — from drawing down stockpiles. The buffer that absorbs the price suppression is what's being exported. Martenson's framing: "The US energy reserves and buffers are being exported at subsidized prices to maintain the illusion that things are not as bad as they actually are."
